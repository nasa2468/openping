# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want OpenPing to be a welcoming and constructive project. Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and professional;
- discuss ideas, code, and behavior without personal attacks;
- accept constructive feedback;
- focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from the project.

If you have concerns about conduct, contact the project maintainer privately through the contact information listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
